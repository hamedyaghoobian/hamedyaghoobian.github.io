The Animals, Counted

Every animal in What the Animals Know, by the mind the text gives it

Read

how much mind, animal by animal

Each bar is one animal in one book, as an animal in the story, a creature or a character, divided by how much mind the text gives it. Beside it, how often the animal is said of a person instead. Press an animal to list its passages below.

who is used for what, and what they are given

The relation to people against the faculties the passage gives the animal: how many in each relation are given speech, reasoning, feeling, cunning. Wagschal's claim is that the first decides the second.

animals said of people

The other direction: the hero as a lion, the army as a dragon, the soul as a bird. How often each animal is a figure of speech rather than an animal, and what share of its appearances that is.

the drawings

The animals are drawn from NaarDoon, Eranshahr's open library of Iranian and Persianate visual elements. Each is a hand tracing of an object in a museum, shown here in one ink, its own outline filled. Credit belongs to the people who traced them and the collections that keep the objects.

The judgements were made by Claude Sonnet 5.5 under the scheme in the code, from the categories of Steven Wagschal, Minding Animals in the Old and New Worlds (Toronto, 2018); 140 drawn at random were read again, 128 agreed, 2 were wrong and corrected, 10 arguable. Texts from Ganjoor.
