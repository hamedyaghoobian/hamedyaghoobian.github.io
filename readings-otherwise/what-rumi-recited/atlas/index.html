The Atlas

The Quran in the Divan-e Shams, for What Rumi Recited: by order, as a network, by what the ayahs are about

Three ways into the same 808 citations. Atlas sets the Quran in its order above the Divan in its order, a line for each cited couplet. Network lets the ayahs and the poems that cite them pull together, so the ayahs heard in many poems gather their poems around them. Themes counts the citations by what the ayahs are about, by the figures and stories they tell, and by how Rumi borrows: in the ayah's words, in Persian, or by its story. Select an ayah or a poem anywhere to trace it; every ayah in the selection gets its own colour.

Citations, Arabic text and Fooladvand's translation come from the reviewed dataset. Items marked AI were written by Claude Opus 5.5 and re-checked by a blind review.

The citations: Saber Soleymani, shams-quran-link, with Hamed Yaghoobian's review and annotations. The themes, figures, relations and glosses (marked AI) were written by Claude Opus 5.5; the 17 links a blind review did not confirm were read again by Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Sonnet 5.5: where they agree the ruling is applied (6 kept, 3 dropped, 2 given a second ayah), and the 6 where they disagree stay flagged. The Quran's text: the Uthmani text of Tanzil, through alquran.cloud; the Persian translation, Mohammad-Mahdi Fooladvand's. The charts use D3.
