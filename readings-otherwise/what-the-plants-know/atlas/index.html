The Plants, Counted

Every plant in What the Plants Know, by the soul the text gives it

Read

how high, plant by plant

Each bar is one plant in one book, as a plant in the story, growing or cut or speaking, divided by the rung of the ladder the text puts it on. Beside it, how often the plant is said of a person instead. Press a plant to list its passages below.

who is used for what, and what they are given

The relation to people against the faculties the passage gives the plant: how many in each relation are given speech, feeling, desire, devotion. Wagschal's claim for animals was that the first decides the second; here is the same test for plants.

plants said of people

The other direction: the beloved as a cypress, a cheek as a rose or a tulip, eyes as narcissi, an enemy as a thorn. How often each plant is a figure of speech rather than a plant, and what share of its appearances that is.

the drawings

The plants are drawn from NaarDoon, Eranshahr's open library of Iranian and Persianate visual elements. Each is a hand tracing of a Sasanian object or a Persian miniature, shown here in one ink, its own outline filled. Credit belongs to the people who traced them and the collections that keep the originals. Plants without a drawing that reads alone (the narcissus, the reed, the plane tree) are shown by their initial.

The judgements were made by Claude Sonnet 5.5 under the scheme in the code, on the ladder of Avicenna's vegetative, animal and rational souls, with the fields of the animals' atlas so the two can be set side by side; 60 drawn at random were read again, 56 agreed, 1 was wrong and corrected, 3 arguable. Texts from Ganjoor.
